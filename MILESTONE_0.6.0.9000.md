# eyeprocess 0.6.0.9000 measurement-intelligence milestone

This development milestone adds a dependency-free reference
implementation for sixteen linked research capabilities:

- probabilistic AOI assignment and uncertainty propagation;
- compositional AOI attention;
- measurement-uncertainty budgets;
- calibration drift and offline recalibration;
- process reliability and Generalizability Theory;
- cross-device metric linking and equivalence;
- pupil phase-amplitude registration;
- informative missingness and MNAR sensitivity;
- recurrence and cross-recurrence analysis;
- experimental fixation point-process models;
- representative scanpaths and dispersion;
- cognitive-episode change-point detection;
- multi-objective item-bank optimization;
- process-DIF and fairness drift;
- conditional process reference centiles; and
- evidence/decision provenance graphs.

All operational, diagnostic, fairness, accessibility, and anomaly
outputs remain review signals rather than automatic causal, clinical, or
misconduct determinations.
